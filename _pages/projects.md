This page presents selected European research projects in which I have been involved as a member of FBK's Digital Humanities group. Click a project title to visit its webpage.

### [AI-CODE ↗](https://aicode-project.eu/)

**AI services for COntinuous trust in emerging Digital Environments**
_Horizon Europe · December 2023 – November 2026_

AI-CODE develops AI-based services to support media professionals in producing trustworthy information and addressing disinformation in emerging digital environments.

Within FBK's Digital Humanities group, I developed models for hate-speech and check-worthiness detection to support the analysis of online content.

### [HATEDEMICS ↗](https://dh.fbk.eu/2025/08/hatedemics-hampering-hate-speech-and-disinformation-through-ai-based-technologies/)

**Hampering hate speech and disinformation through AI-based technologies**
_Citizens, Equality, Rights and Values Programme · April 2024 – March 2026_

HATEDEMICS aimed to strengthen preventive and reactive measures against online hate speech and disinformation by developing AI-based technologies for monitoring harmful content and supporting targeted counter-narrative interventions.

Within FBK's Digital Humanities group, I was responsible for the project's analysis of social media data collected from Telegram. This work included hate-speech and check-worthiness detection, topic modelling, and emotion detection to investigate the characteristics and distribution of harmful and potentially misleading content.

**Selected publication**

- [MuLTa-Telegram: A Fine-Grained Italian and Polish Dataset for Hate Speech and Target Detection](https://aclanthology.org/2025.clicit-1.56/), _CLiC-it 2025_.

### [StandByMe ↗](https://dh.fbk.eu/2023/01/stand-by-me-project-fight-violence-against-women/)

**Stop online violence against women and girls by changing attitudes and behaviour of young people through human rights education**
_Rights, Equality and Citizenship Programme · December 2021 – December 2023_

StandByMe aimed to counter online violence against women and girls by educating and empowering young people to recognise it and take action.

Within FBK's Digital Humanities group, I contributed to the analysis of misogynistic language on social media and to the development of NLP methods for its automatic detection. This work supported the project's digital tools and educational activities for raising awareness of online gender-based violence.

**Selected publications**

- [Real Men are Tough: Evaluating Gender Bias and Sensitivity to Masculinity Norms in LLMs](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.225/), _Findings of ACL 2026_.
- [DH-FBK at SemEval-2023 Task 10: Multi-Task Learning with Classifier Ensemble Agreement for Sexism Detection](https://aclanthology.org/2023.semeval-1.261/), _SemEval 2023_.
- [DH-FBK at HODI: Multi-Task Learning with Classifier Ensemble Agreement, Oversampling and Synthetic Data](https://aclanthology.org/2023.evalita-1.34/), _EVALITA 2023_.

### [ODEUROPA ↗](https://odeuropa.eu/)

**Negotiating Olfactory and Sensory Experiences in Cultural Heritage Practice and Research**
_Horizon 2020 · January 2021 – December 2023_

ODEUROPA used artificial intelligence to identify and reconstruct smells, olfactory practices, and sensory experiences represented in European cultural heritage collections.

Within FBK's Digital Humanities group, I worked on the automatic extraction of olfactory information from historical texts and the analysis of changes in how smells have been described and perceived over time.

**Selected publication**

- [Scent and Sensibility: Perception Shifts in the Olfactory Domain](https://aclanthology.org/2023.lchange-1.15/), _LChange 2023_.

### [PERCEPTIONS ↗](https://dh.fbk.eu/2020/11/perceptions-identify-and-understand-narratives-imaginations-and-mis-perceptions-of-the-eu/)

**Understanding the Impact of Narratives and Perceptions of Europe on Migration and Providing Practices, Tools and Guides for Practitioners**
_Horizon 2020 · September 2019 – February 2023_

PERCEPTIONS investigated how Europe and the EU are perceived by migrants and prospective migrants, how related narratives circulate through social and traditional media, and how distorted information may influence migration decisions.

Within FBK's Digital Humanities group, I contributed to the collection and analysis of social media data concerning Europe and migration. My work examined the topics and narratives circulating online and their patterns of diffusion across geographical and linguistic communities using network analysis, user geolocation, and multilingual content analysis.

**Selected publications**

- [The Geography of Information Diffusion in Online Discourse on Europe and Migration](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/ICWSM/article/view/31361), _ICWSM 2024_.
- [Social Media and Migration Studies](https://researchportal.northumbria.ac.uk/en/publications/social-media-and-migration-studies/), _CHI 2023_.
- [Social Media Narratives on Europe and Migration: Seen from an Outside Europe Point of View](https://www.perceptions.eu/social-media-narratives-on-europe-and-migration-seen-from-an-outside-europe-point-of-view/), _PERCEPTIONS Policy Brief, 2023_.
