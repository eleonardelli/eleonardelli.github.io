## Current project

### [AI-CODE](https://aicode-project.eu/)

**AI services for COntinuous trust in emerging Digital Environments**  
_Horizon Europe · December 2023 – November 2026_

AI-CODE develops AI-based services to support media professionals in producing trustworthy information and addressing disinformation in emerging digital environments.

At FBK, I contribute to the development of content-driven services, with a focus on semantic analysis and NLP-based tools for analysing online content.

## Previous projects

### [HATEMETER](http://hatemeter.eu/)

**Hate speech tool for monitoring, analysing and tackling anti-Muslim hatred online**  
_Rights, Equality and Citizenship Programme · February 2018 – January 2020_

HATEMETER developed an ICT platform for automatically monitoring and analysing anti-Muslim hate speech online and supporting NGOs in creating counter-narratives and awareness-raising campaigns.

My work concerned the collection and computational analysis of social media data and the development of NLP methods for identifying and studying anti-Muslim hate speech.

### [StandByMe](https://www.standbymeproject.eu/)

**Stop online violence against women and girls by changing attitudes and behaviour of young people through human rights education**  
_Rights, Equality and Citizenship Programme · December 2021 – December 2023_

StandByMe aimed to counter online violence against women and girls by educating and empowering young people to recognise it and take action.

My work focused on the analysis of misogynistic language online and on NLP methods supporting digital tools and educational activities for raising awareness of gender-based violence.

### [ODEUROPA](https://odeuropa.eu/)

**Negotiating Olfactory and Sensory Experiences in Cultural Heritage Practice and Research**  
_Horizon 2020 · January 2021 – December 2023_

ODEUROPA used artificial intelligence to identify and reconstruct the smells, olfactory practices, and sensory experiences represented in European cultural heritage collections.

My research explored the automatic extraction of olfactory information from historical texts and the analysis of changes in how smells have been described and perceived over time.
