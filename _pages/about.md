I'm **Elisa Leonardelli**, a researcher in Natural Language Processing with a background in cognitive neuroscience and bioengineering. I currently work as a Postdoctoral Researcher in the <strong><a href="https://dh.fbk.eu/">Digital Humanities group</a></strong> at <strong><a href="https://www.fbk.eu/en/">Fondazione Bruno Kessler</a></strong> (FBK) in Trento, Italy.

My research focuses on subjective and socially grounded language phenomena, including hate speech, disagreement, perspectivism, and bias in language models. I am particularly interested in how different human perspectives can be represented in NLP datasets and reflected in the evaluation of language technologies. Alongside this research, I contribute to European projects concerning AI, online discourse, and social impact.
